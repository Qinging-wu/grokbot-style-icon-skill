# Reusable generation prompt

Adapt the bracketed identity fields to the current reference. Keep the invariant design rules unless the user gives a newer, conflicting instruction.

```text
Redraw the current character reference as a minimalist, cute, recognizable 2D mascot icon in a square 1:1 composition.

CURRENT CHARACTER — [hair color and silhouette; distinctive structural locks; at most one or two defining accessories; exact source-matched skin lightness and undertone; a small piece of the original collar/scarf/bow/uniform at the bottom edge]. Use only the current identity reference for these details. Do not carry over traits from any previous character or from a composition-only reference.

FACE AND HEAD ROLL — The face looks directly at the viewer, fully frontal and making eye contact, without yaw, three-quarter view, perspective, or near/far facial distortion. Roll the complete head group (head, face, hair, eyes, blush, and accessories together) about 15 degrees clockwise in the image plane. Verify the screen direction: the image-left eye is higher than the image-right eye; the eye-center line slopes down from left to right. Never reverse this direction. In face-local coordinates, keep both eyes vertical, equal in size, symmetric, and parallel; they rotate with the face rather than being straightened independently.

COMPOSITION — Square extreme close-up. Place the face in the lower-left, not centered; let hair and face crop naturally at the left and bottom edges. Keep both eyes fully visible. Reserve a clean, continuous dark charcoal/near-black region in the upper-right, roughly one-fifth to one-quarter of the canvas. Keep hair, accessories, and shoulders out of this region.

FACE MARKS — Use exactly two matching solid-black rounded capsule eyes, about 3:1 height-to-width, with no iris, pupil, sclera, highlight, eyeliner, eyebrow, or lashes. Add one soft pink oval blush to each cheek below the eyes; move each mark slightly outward toward its own cheek, but keep it away from the face edge and hair. Keep the marks similar in size and color, parallel in face-local coordinates, and rotated together with the face. No nose, mouth, or extra face marks unless requested.

SKIN — Match the source face and visible neck in lightness and undertone. Do not normalize to pale cream; do not bleach darker skin or darken, yellow, peach, orange, or pink-shift very fair skin. Use a restrained, even flat base with light shading; keep blush separate from the base skin color.

CLOTHING — Show a short neck, a small amount of shoulder, and a recognizable fragment of the current character’s own collar, lapel, bow, scarf, or uniform at the bottom edge. The character must read as dressed. Do not erase the clothing, expose bare shoulders, or expand into a bust or torso portrait.

RENDERING — Soft matte, mostly flat 2D anime mascot coloring. Preserve the current character’s hair silhouette and distinctive strand structure; simplify into a few coherent shapes. Use no more than one or two identity-defining accessories. Plain dark charcoal background, no texture, frame, card, text, logo, watermark, particles, weapons, hands, full body, 3D realism, heavy outline, or elaborate shading.

Before finishing, check source-matched skin, frontal direct gaze, clockwise roll with image-left eye higher, eyes parallel in face coordinates, lower-left placement, clear upper-right space, outward cheek blush, visible clothing, and the current character’s distinctive hair structure.
```
