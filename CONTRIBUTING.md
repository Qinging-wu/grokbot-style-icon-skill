# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this workflow.

## What belongs in the shared rules

- A repeatable failure mode that appeared across more than one character or was explicitly confirmed as a general preference belongs in `references/style-spec.md` and the final visual check in `SKILL.md` when it changes the workflow.
- A one-character trait belongs only in that generation’s filled prompt. Do not make a particular hair color, clip, skin tone, or outfit a universal requirement.
- When a user says the rest is good, preserve those approved properties and keep the revision narrowly scoped.

## Reference images

Do not submit copyrighted character screenshots or generated outputs unless their redistribution is authorized. When an image is necessary to explain a regression, use original, synthetic, or openly licensed material and identify its source and license.

## Suggested change notes

Describe the observed failure, which rule should change, and how the proposed wording prevents that failure without weakening other constraints. Keep the skill entrypoint concise; place substantial guidance in the relevant reference file.
